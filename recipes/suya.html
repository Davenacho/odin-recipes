Suya Skewers

Home

Ingredients

  • Beef, chicken, or lamb, thinly sliced
  • Roasted peanuts, ground
  • Ginger powder
  • Cayenne pepper
  • Paprika
  • Onion powder
  • Vegetable oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Grind roasted peanuts into a coarse powder to make the suya spice base.
  2. Mix the ground peanuts with ginger powder, cayenne pepper, paprika, onion powder, and salt.
  3. Thread the thin slices of meat onto skewers.
  4. Brush each skewer lightly with vegetable oil.
  5. Coat the skewers generously with the suya spice mix, pressing it into the meat.
  6. Let the skewers rest for at least 30 minutes so the spice can soak in.
  7. Grill the skewers over medium-high heat, turning occasionally.
  8. Brush with a little more oil while grilling to keep the meat from drying out.
  9. Cook until the meat is done and lightly charred on the edges.
  10. Serve and enjoy
