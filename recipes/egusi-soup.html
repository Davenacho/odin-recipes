Egusi soup

Home

Ingredients

A hearty Nigerian soup made from ground melon seeds, thickened into a savory stew with leafy greens, meat, and fish. Usually served with fufu, pounded yam, or rice.

  • Ground egusi (melon seeds)
  • Palm oil
  • Crayfish
  • Vegetables (spinach or ugu)
  • Stockfish or dried fish
  • Stock cubes and seasoning<

Steps

  1. Cook the meat and stockfish with onions, seasoning, and stock cubes until tender.
  2. Heat palm oil in a pot and fry chopped onions until fragrant.
  3. Mix ground egusi with a little water to form a paste, then add to the pot in spoonfuls.
  4. Allow the egusi to fry gently, stirring occasionally, until it forms soft lumps.
  5. Stir in ground crayfish and pepper for extra flavor.
  6. Pour in the meat stock and stir to loosen the mixture into a thick soup.
  7. Add the cooked meat and stockfish to the pot.
  8. Simmer for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally so it doesn't stick.
  9. Add the leafy vegetables and cook for a few more minutes until wilted.
  10. Serve and enjoy
