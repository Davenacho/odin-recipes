Jellof Rice

A smoky, spiced West African rice dish cooked in a rich tomato and pepper sauce. It's a staple at parties and celebrations, known for its deep color and bold flavor.

Home

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Onions
  • vegetable Oil
  • Chicken or beef

Steps

  1. Blend tomatoes, red bell peppers, onions, and scotch bonnet peppers into a smooth puree.
  2. Fry the puree in oil with tomato paste until the raw smell fades and the oil separates.
  3. Add salt, curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, and stock cubes to season the sauce.
  4. Pour in stock or water to thin the sauce to the right consistency.
  5. Rinse the rice under cold water until it runs clear.
  6. Stir the rice into the sauce until evenly coated and mostly submerged.
  7. Cover the pot and simmer on low heat, stirring occasionally.
  8. Check the rice and add small amounts of water if it dries out before it's done.
  9. Let the rice sit covered off the heat for a few minutes to finish steaming.
  10. Serve and enjoy
